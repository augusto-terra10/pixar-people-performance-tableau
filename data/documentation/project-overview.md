# Project Overview

## The People Behind Pixar’s Success

This Tableau project explores the creative talent behind Pixar’s films and their box office performance.

The dashboard allows users to explore contributors by creative role, examine their relationship with film performance, and discover the Pixar films associated with an individual contributor.

## Dashboard Experience

- **Explore by Creative Role:** Select a creative role to focus the analysis.
- **Creative Talent & Box Office Performance:** Explore contributors and their film box office performance in the scatter plot.
- **Explore Films by Contributor:** Select a person in the chart to view the films they contributed to.
- **Film contributor details:** Hover over a film poster to view its creative contributors.

## Key Metrics

The dashboard currently highlights:

- People: 95
- Films: 28
- Awards Won: 18
- Average Worldwide Box Office per Film: $608.7M

These figures reflect the values presented in the dashboard and should be updated if the underlying data or calculations change.
