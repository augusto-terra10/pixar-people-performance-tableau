# Pixar People & Performance

## Project Overview

An interactive Tableau dashboard exploring the creative talent behind Pixar’s films and their box office performance.  The dashboard connects creative contributors, film portfolios, production budgets and worldwide box office results, allowing users to explore the people behind Pixar’s most successful films.

## Dashboard Preview

![Pixar People & Performance](screenshots/Employee%20Transfer%20%26%20Financial%20Analysis.png)

## Key Takeways

This dashboard highlights the relationship between Pixar’s creative talent and the performance of its films, providing an interactive way to explore contributor portfolios and film-level box office results.

## Key Features

- Creative Role Exploration – Explore contributors by creative role, including producers, directors, co-directors, screenwriters, storywriters and musicians.
- Contributor Performance Analysis – Compare creative contributors based on the average budget and worldwide box office of their film portfolios.
- Film Portfolio Exploration – Select a contributor to explore the Pixar films they worked on.
- Interactive Film Posters – Hover over a film poster to view its creative contributors and roles.
- KPI Overview – Track the number of contributors, films, awards won and average worldwide box office per film.

## Key Metrics

- People: 95
- Films: 28
- Awards Won: 18
- Avg. Worldwide Box Office per Film: $608.7M

## Data and Model

The project uses multiple datasets to connect Pixar films with their creative contributors, financial performance, public response, genres and awards.

Datasets include:
- Pixar People
- Pixar Films
- Box Office
- Academy Awards
- Public Response
- Genres

The data model supports exploration across films, contributors and their creative roles.
